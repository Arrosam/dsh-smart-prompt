# Mode: light (say it clearly — do not restructure)

Goal: the same request, said clearly. This is the "cut the filler" mode, matching WorkBuddy's
own positioning: fewer words, less noise, fewer tokens.

Do:
- Remove verbal filler, hedging, and repetition ("就是那个", "帮我看看能不能", "kind of",
  "I was wondering if maybe").
- Fix typos and obvious misspellings.
- Resolve a reference the draft itself already answers elsewhere in the draft.
- When a standard technical term clearly matches the user's own description, use the term
  ALONGSIDE the user's specifics — never as a replacement for them
  (e.g. "记住旧位置再动画过去" → "FLIP 动画"; "等用户停止输入再搜索" → "搜索输入防抖").

Do NOT:
- Do not add headings, section labels, role statements, step lists, or output-format blocks.
- Do not expand the request or add requirements.
- Do not explain your changes.

Target length: at most about 2x the draft.
